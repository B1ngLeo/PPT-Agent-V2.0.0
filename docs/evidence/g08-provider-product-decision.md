This record closes the Product, Security, and Legal human approval for the selected models,
gateway, customer disclosure, unit-based cost accounting, and the bounded product use

model documentation now lists `gpt-image-2` for image generation and editing. The current

The project owner supplied both variable names through a local secret file. The original

`docs/evidence/security/g08-live-provider-smoke.json`. Do not paste either key into this
document, logs, evidence, prompts, browser bundles, or container image layers.

The integrated synthetic product-path check subsequently passed strict Kimi intent and
four-slide outline planning plus a GPT Image 2 cover compiled into an editable PPTX with
exactly one referenced media part and no full-slide bitmap fallback. Generated bytes and
prompt/response content were deleted with the temporary workspace; redacted evidence is in
`docs/evidence/security/g08-live-product-provider-integration.json`.

## Approved product use and accepted risk

- Kimi `kimi-k3` may receive the presentation topic, intent and outline instructions for
  intent/outline planning through the selected gateway.
- OpenAI `gpt-image-2` may receive a derived, text-free cover-art prompt. The product is
  capped at one generated cover image per deck; it does not send source documents to the
  image endpoint.
- Product usage accounting records Kimi input/output token units and an image quantity of
  zero or one per publication. Monetary reconciliation remains dependent on the gateway's
  actual rate card and invoice.
- The owner accepts that `cf.api.fan` is a third-party processor in front of the upstream
  suppliers. Its processing region, upstream routing, retention, training/abuse review,
  subprocessors, deletion guarantees and supplier terms were not independently verified
  from authoritative public material during this review. Approval is an explicit risk
  acceptance, not evidence that those claims were verified.
- The customer-facing disclosure in `docs/privacy-and-provider-disclosure.md` is approved
  for this product path.

This human approval does **not** assert that production KES/KMS is deployed. The later
[local-only scope decision](g08-local-only-release-scope.md) defers that control only while
the project remains owner-operated and local. External, shared, hosted, QA, staging or
production use remains blocked until the deployment-specific KES/KMS control has machine
evidence and Security sign-off. The unrelated Windows Chromium 200% plus screen-reader
review also remains open.
